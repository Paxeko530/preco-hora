Typical costs for a independent consultant

  • Travel and accommodation for client visits
  • Training, certifications and industry events
  • Management and remote-meeting software
  • Professional liability insurance
  • Accountant and legal help with contracts

Add up your real costs and enter the monthly total in the fixed costs field.

Where billable time goes missing

Consultative selling is slow: exploratory calls, tailored proposals and relationship building take months before they produce revenue.

How to set your price

Diagnostics and projects get a fixed price; ongoing support is billed as a retainer or as pre-agreed days of work.

  • Charge for the value you deliver to the client, not just the hours spent.
  • Avoid long free proposals: limit the initial diagnosis.
  • Contracts with scope, deadlines and staged payments protect you from constant changes.

Can I charge more than my minimum rate?

You should. The minimum is the floor below which you lose money; the selling price should reflect the value to the client.
