Typical costs for a tutor and trainer

  • Materials, textbooks and video lesson platforms
  • A digital whiteboard or tablet
  • Travel, if you teach at students' homes
  • Accountant
  • Ongoing teaching training

Add up your real costs and enter the monthly total in the fixed costs field.

Where billable time goes missing

Preparing lessons, marking work, rescheduling sessions and finding students take many hours a week. Demand is concentrated in certain times of the year.

How to set your price

Individual lessons are billed per session. Corporate training can be priced per day or per group.

  • The lesson is what you bill, but preparation and travel must be reflected in the price.
  • Set a clear cancellation policy.
  • Sell monthly lesson packages: they reduce no-shows and give you stability.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I just charge the lesson time?

Because preparation, travel and unfilled slots reduce your real income per hour.

How do I handle cancellations?

Set a minimum notice period in writing and charge for the lesson if it is not respected.
