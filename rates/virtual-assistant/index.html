Where billable time goes missing

Switching context between several clients, managing expectations and handling urgent requests cuts into truly productive time.

How to set your price

Monthly hour banks are the norm. Specialist services (inbox management, scheduling) can have their own price.

  • Sell monthly hour packages with an expiry period.
  • Bill in 15- or 30-minute increments, with a minimum per request.
  • Avoid clients who demand instant availability without paying for it.

Frequently asked questions

Should I charge hourly or by package?

Monthly hour packages give you predictability; ad-hoc hourly billing suits new clients.

Can I charge for urgent requests?

Yes. Set a surcharge for requests outside the agreed hours.
