  • Wear and replacement of cameras, lenses and lighting
  • Memory cards, drives and backups

Add up your real costs and enter the monthly total in the fixed costs field.

Where billable time goes missing

For every hour shooting there are several of culling, editing, delivery and client communication. The billable share tends to be lower than in other professions.

How to set your price

Events and portraits sell as packages with hours of coverage and delivered photos. Commercial photography combines a day rate with a usage license.

  • Count editing as working time: it often exceeds the time spent shooting.
  • Charge for travel and waiting time at events.
  • Sell packages with a set number of edited photos so clients can compare value.

Frequently asked questions

Why use a low billable percentage?

Because editing, file management and finding clients take up a large part of your time and are not billed separately.

Should I charge for editing separately?

You can include it in the package or bill it hourly for jobs that need heavy retouching.
