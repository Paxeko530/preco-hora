Typical costs for a freelance developer

  • Hosting, domains and paid developer tools
  • IDE licenses, project management and private version control
  • A powerful work computer and a second monitor
  • Courses and time spent learning to stay current
  • Accountant and professional liability insurance

Add up your real costs and enter the monthly total in the fixed costs field.

Where billable time goes missing

Non-billable time comes from requirements discovery, the weekly client call, post-delivery support and technical debt inherited from other people's code.

How to set your price

Fixed-price projects with milestone payments work well. For ongoing work, a monthly retainer with included hours protects both sides.

  • Always estimate with a buffer for integrations and surprises; software projects tend to overrun.
  • Define what counts as maintenance and what counts as a new feature before you deliver.
  • Prefer support retainers to one-off requests: they make your income predictable.

Frequently asked questions

Should I charge hourly or per project?

Use the hourly rate to find your minimum and for small requests. On larger projects, a fixed price with milestone payments reduces disputes.

How do I charge for support after delivery?

Set a warranty period for bugs, then a monthly retainer or an hours package for everything else.
