Typical costs for a graphic designer

  • Design software subscriptions and font licenses
  • Stock images and asset licenses for client projects
  • A computer and a calibrated monitor, replaced every 3–4 years
  • Cloud storage for heavy files
  • Accountant and professional liability insurance, where relevant

Add up your real costs and enter the monthly total in the fixed costs field.

Where billable time goes missing

Unlimited revisions are the biggest drain: every extra round of changes is time the client does not pay for. Add briefing calls and preparing final files for delivery.

How to set your price

Logo and identity projects sell best as fixed packages. Social media and recurring materials work better as a monthly retainer.

  • Cap revision rounds in the quote (two is common) and bill extra rounds hourly.
  • Charge for usage rights separately from the creative work.
  • A logo and a full brand identity are not the same job: sell packages, not loose hours.

Frequently asked questions

Should I bill logo revisions hourly?

Include a fixed number of rounds in the project price and bill additional rounds hourly. It prevents endless revisions.

Do I charge for usage rights separately?

It is common to define the scope of use in the contract and charge more for wider use, such as advertising or resale.
