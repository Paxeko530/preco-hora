Where billable time goes missing

Replying to messages, following trends and justifying results to the client are ongoing tasks that rarely appear in the original quote.

How to set your price

A monthly retainer is the natural model. Charge for ad management and video production as separate services.

  • Sell retainers with a set number of posts and interactions included.
  • Define what is out of scope: paid ads, out-of-hours replies, photo production.
  • Send a simple monthly report: it justifies the retainer and reduces cancellations.
