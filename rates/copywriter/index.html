Where billable time goes missing

Research, interviews, client-requested revisions and the constant search for new work take far more time than the writing itself.

How to set your price

Blog posts can be priced per piece, sales pages and emails per project, and regular content as a monthly retainer.

  • Price research in: an 800-word article rarely takes only the time it takes to write.
  • Sell by deliverable (article, sales page) rather than per word when the work is strategic.
  • Limit revisions and always ask for a written brief.

Frequently asked questions

Should I charge per word or per article?

A per-article or per-project price reflects the research involved. A per-word price suits simple, high-volume orders.

How many revisions should I include?

Two rounds are usually reasonable. Put it in writing before you start.
